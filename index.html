About

Engineering that answers to people, not just to benchmarks

Assalamu Alaikum. I'm Kaiser Mohiuddin, born in Kupwara, Jammu & Kashmir, and currently in my 7th semester of B.Tech Computer Science Engineering at Baba Ghulam Shah Badshah University, Rajouri — batch 2023–2027.

My work sits at the intersection of applied machine learning and product engineering. I care less about models that look good in a notebook and more about systems people actually depend on: a RAG assistant that answers in under two seconds, a forensic pipeline that flags a forged document and can explain why, an app that keeps working when the network drops.

That instinct comes from where I grew up. Technology in the valley has to be resilient and worth the trust placed in it. For me it is an amanah — something held in trust — so I try to build things that are transparent about what they know, honest about what they don't, and useful to the person on the other side of the screen.

Outside of code I play chess and cricket, spend time with the Qur'an, and mentor juniors at university on getting their first ML project past the tutorial stage.

Kashmiri Sentiment Analysis

Sentiment classification for Roman-transliterated Kashmiri, developed during the NIT Srinagar research internship. The hard part is the input: no standard spelling, heavy code-mixing with Urdu and English, and almost no labelled data. The pipeline handles normalisation and TF-IDF featurisation, with a Linear SVM baseline served over Flask.

License Plate Recognition & Blacklist Verification

Real-time ANPR built on a custom-trained YOLOv8 detector with Tesseract OCR for plate reading. Recognised plates are checked against a watchlist using fuzzy matching, so a single misread character doesn't cause a miss — the practical failure mode in field conditions with dirt, glare and motion blur.
